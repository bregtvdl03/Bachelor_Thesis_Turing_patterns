In this chapter, we will discuss a bulk-surface model that describes the interactions of ligands and cell receptors. We will give a proof of the existence and uniqueness of weak solutions to this model, as well as discuss some of the qualitative properties of such solutions. The proof will rely heavily on the definitions and results introduced in \cref{ch:Preliminaries}, and will be based on an application of Banach's fixed-point theorem.
